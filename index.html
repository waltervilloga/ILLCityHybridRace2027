Sub-40–50 Min Race-Day Execution Blueprint

  • 1. Smart Set Breaking (Never Go to Muscular Failure) Going unbroken early causes redlining that costs minutes later. Break reps intentionally from Station 01: e.g., 4 × 10 on Plate Ground-to-Overhead, 2 × 20 on Box Step Ups, and 5 × 6 or 6 × 5 on Hand Release Push-Ups with strict 5–8 second rests.
  • 2. Step-Up Burpees on Station 03 (40m Burpee Broad Jumps) Step one foot up at a time out of the burpee instead of jumping both feet forward—this saves massive hip-flexor and lower-back energy while keeping your heart rate under control.
  • 3. Grip Management on Stations 04 & 06 On the 40 DB Suitcase Deadlifts (35 lbs), do sets of 10–15 with a quick 5-second shakeout. On the 120m Farmers Carry (24 kg), plan 2–3 brief 5-second drops before your forearms lock up so you can pick the kettlebells right back up.
  • 4. Steady Rhythm on Sandbag Lunges & 60 Wallballs Take a 10-second breath before hoisting the 20 kg Sandbag, then take smooth, controlled steps without rushing. On the final 60 Wallballs (14 lbs), break into manageable sets of 10–12 reps with 5–6 seconds rest between sets.
  • 5. Keep Moving on Every 400m Run Even if your legs feel heavy after lunges or deadlifts, avoid walking the 400m segments. Start each run with a light 50m shuffle to catch your breath, then settle into a steady 2:10–2:20/lap jog.
